On 21 March 2023, our Chairman Wolfgang Döbler and Treasurer Konrad Wich stepped down from their roles due to health and age reasons.

Wolfgang Döbler has been a member of the club since 1969 and took over as Chairman of FC Treptow e.V. in 2009. Konrad Wich has been a member since 1967 and Treasurer since 2007.

Both will remain with the club as honorary council and audit committee members on the board.
