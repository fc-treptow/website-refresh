On 28 June 2025, we wrapped up the 2024/25 season at our club grounds. This year’s event was dedicated to a special memorial tournament in honour of Wolfgang Döbler, who passed away on 19 December 2024.

Wolfgang shaped the club for decades, as a player, long-time chairman, and later as honorary chairman. His dedication and loyalty made him a key figure at FC Treptow. Former players from our club came together to remember him in a sporting way and played against our current teams: Men's I, Men's III, the Recreational Kickers, the former 40s and other former players.

After a minute of silence, we played, grilled and reminisced. The trophy was won by FC Treptow’s Men's I.

We thank everyone who took part and made our tournament a special experience. Your commitment, passion and fair play shaped this day.

The Board
