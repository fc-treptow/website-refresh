At the end of the 2022/23 season, Wolfgang Döbler, our long-standing first chairman, honorary member and honorary council member of FC Treptow, was appointed Honorary Chairman in recognition of his outstanding services.

As Honorary Chairman, he has the right to attend board meetings and may be entrusted by the board with tasks on behalf of the club.

Congratulations!
