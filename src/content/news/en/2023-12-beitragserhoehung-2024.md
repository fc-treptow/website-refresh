Resolution of the extraordinary general meeting from 07.12.2023, valid from 01.01.2024:

- **Active members (full payers):** €15.00 per month (€180.00 per year), €165.00 if paid annually
- **Active members (reduced):** Trainees, students, citizens receiving benefits, recreational players, youths: €10.00 per month (€120.00 per year), €110.00 if paid annually
- **Passive members:** €6.00 per month

The currently valid fees are always listed under [Membership](/mitgliedschaft).
