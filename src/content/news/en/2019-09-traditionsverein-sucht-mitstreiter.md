**Traditional Club Seeks New Teammates**

**FC Treptow also offers older players a football home on Thursdays around 6 pm at the Willi-Sänger-Sportanlage on Köpenicker Landstraße. Wolfgang Döbler sits in the former gatehouse at the entrance to the pitch. Every now and then a player pokes their head through the door and greets him warmly.**

Because today is training night at FC Treptow and the gatehouse serves as the club’s office. The club has only existed since 1994, but the football tradition goes much further back. “Our predecessor was founded in 1925 as Turbine Berlin, a company club for Bewag employees. Over the following years the name changed several times, but the word Turbine and the connection to Bewag and later the energy combine remained loyal to the club,” explains Wolfgang Döbler. In the founding year of the current club in 1994, footballers from ESV Lok Schöneweide – also founded in 1925 by railway workers – joined, and since 1996 players from the former SV Narva have strengthened the squad.

“We play with our 1st team in Kreisliga A and have around 160 active players. Our club is a member of the Berlin Football Association. We travel all over Berlin for league matches,” says chairman Döbler. The 3rd men's team also includes residents from a nearby refugee shelter.

In Berlin, FC Treptow is one of the smaller clubs that have to fight for every sponsor. “We could really use support because a full set of kit for one team quickly costs between 800 and 1200 euros,” says Wolfgang Döbler.

At the moment, the club has to limit itself to the artificial pitch equipped with floodlights, as the grass pitch on Köpenicker Landstraße is reserved exclusively for VSG Altglienicke (Regionalliga) due to planned renovation work at their home stadium. FC Treptow currently has no youth section. That’s why they’re looking for new players aged 18 to 60. “But we also have room for beginners or those returning to football after their working life,” promises Wolfgang Döbler.

Anyone who wants to play for FC Treptow can simply come to training on Tuesday or Thursday from 6 pm. On Thursdays from 6 to 8 pm, you’ll also find the chairman in his office in the gatehouse at the sports ground, Köpenicker Landstraße 186.
