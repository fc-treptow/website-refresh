On paper, the roles were clear. Sparta Lichtenberg II play in the Bezirksliga, our Men's II two divisions lower in Kreisliga B. But anyone who was at the floodlit match on the Willi-Sänger-Sportanlage on Sunday wouldn’t have noticed that. River matched the favourites on equal terms and ultimately won deservedly and convincingly.

The spectators were treated to plenty of action: all four of River’s goals were real eye-catchers. Martin Kaled Molina Badra and Giuliano Brunetti each scored a brace. The visitors’ temporary equaliser was calmly absorbed by the team, who then held on to their lead.

On the sidelines was interim coach Jannis Rußkamp, who found exactly the right response with his team against the favoured opponents.

By reaching the quarter-finals, our Men's II have shown what they’re made of and that different rules apply in the cup after all. Congratulations to the team on this strong performance!
