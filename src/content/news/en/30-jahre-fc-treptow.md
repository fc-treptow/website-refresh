The 2023/24 season finale was crowned on 15 June 2024 with a celebration for FC Treptow’s 30th anniversary (founded on 19 May 1994). It was a day full of highlights: an exciting football tournament and the honouring of our most loyal members.

For the tournament, our chairman invited not only our current players but also many former FC Treptow players. The reunion was warm and full of nostalgic moments. In total, seven teams competed against each other.

As a club, it was especially important for us to honour our long-standing members. Dieter Lischitzki, Manfred Golke and Fred Ziemann were awarded the honorary shield for 70 years of loyalty. Alexander Otte and Björn Berg received recognition for ten years of membership, and Marco Schmiege was honoured with the bronze badge of honour for his services to the club.

Here’s to many more successful years!
