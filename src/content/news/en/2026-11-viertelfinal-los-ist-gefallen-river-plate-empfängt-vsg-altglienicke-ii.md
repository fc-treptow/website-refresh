After the 4-1 upset against the district league side Sparta Lichtenberg II, the next opponent is set. VSG Altglienicke II.

It won’t be any easier this time. VSG’s reserve currently leads the Landesliga and is the clear favourite. But Sparta was on paper too. The lads have shown they can hold their own against higher league teams, and as we all know, cup matches have their own rules.

By the way, VSG isn’t a stranger from far away. Their first team from the Regionalliga Nordost already trains at our ground, and the district plans to develop the Willi-Sänger-Sportanlage into their future stadium. But on 28 March, we’re the hosts first and foremost.

So save the date and come down to Köpenicker Landstraße. The lads deserve your support!

**Quarter-final Cosy Wasch State Cup 2nd Men**
FC Treptow II River Plate – VSG Altglienicke II
Sunday, 28 March 2027, kick-off time to follow,
Willi-Sänger-Sportanlage
