For the 2022/23 season, the club is registering two new teams:

- the new Men's II, Kreisliga C, Staffel 6
- the new 7-a-side Men's II (River Plate), Kreisliga C, Staffel 2

## River Plate Berlin

These are the lions of the team FC Treptow 7-a-side II / River Plate Soccer 7, representing us in Kreisliga C and the Berlin Cup.

River Plate Berlin introduces itself: We are a place of meeting and community where fans of Club Atlético River Plate, who live in or visit Berlin, come together. We watch football matches together, organise sociocultural gatherings and sporting and solidarity activities: football, padel tennis, volleyball, jogging, barbecues and meet-ups in the many parks and lakes of the city.

Any occasion is good to come together and chat about the great River Plate. The branch is a little piece of River and Argentina in Berlin, so you feel right at home.
