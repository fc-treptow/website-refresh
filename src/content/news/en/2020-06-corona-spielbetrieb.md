## 27 June 2020: New Infection Protection Regulation

On Tuesday, 23 June 2020, the Berlin Senate passed the Infection Protection Regulation. It replaces the previously valid containment regulation and has been in effect since Saturday, 27 June 2020. It also affects football clubs: in Berlin, only contactless sports are still permitted.

## 12 March 2020: BFV match operations paused

The coordination group of the Berlin Football Association regarding the impact of the coronavirus on Berlin amateur football and the executive board decided on Thursday, 12 March 2020, to suspend match operations initially until Sunday, 22 March 2020, inclusive. This applies to all matches under the responsibility of the BFV.
