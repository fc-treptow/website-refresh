Our board member and chief treasurer Konrad Wich was recognised with the DFB Volunteer Award for his work on the conversion, extension and design of the facade of the former gatehouse into a club-owned club and coaches' office, carried out over many voluntary hours.



This DFB Volunteer Award was announced for the 22nd time in March 2019 for the year 2018 by the German Football Association and is thus an established part of the DFB Volunteer Campaign.
