Dear members and friends,

The past year 2021, like 2020, was mainly shaped by restrictions in match operations (Corona). So the quite justified hope remains that in the coming year 2022 everything will get better and the ball will roll more often again.

Before that, however, the Christmas holidays and the turn of the year are coming up. We want to spend both in peace and quiet, so that we can then complete the 2021/22 season. We will have a lot to catch up on next year.

With this in mind, we wish you and your loved ones a Merry Christmas, good health, and all the best.

The Board of FC Treptow e.V.
