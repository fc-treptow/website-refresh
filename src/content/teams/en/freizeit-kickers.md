We're a recreational team that meets once a week for a kickabout. Fed up with tough training drills and prefer to spend your weekend without league matches? Then you've come to the right place.

We play on Tuesdays from 6:00 to 7:30 pm on the artificial turf at the Willi-Sänger-Sportanlage. There's no selection process with us. Just get in touch with the team captain briefly before your first time.
